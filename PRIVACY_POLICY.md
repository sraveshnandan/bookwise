# Privacy Policy for BookWise

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to BookWise ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, BookWise (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time and for any reason. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this Privacy Policy. You are encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy to stay informed of updates.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, password (hashed), profile picture
- **Reading Preferences**: Favorite genres, reading goals, notification settings, font/theme preferences
- **Subscription Information**: Purchase receipts, subscription status, payment method tokens (handled by Apple/Google)
- **Content You Create**: Highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading goals, reviews, ratings

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: Books viewed, reading progress, time spent reading/listening, search queries
- **Device Information**: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers, IP address
- **App Performance**: Crash reports, loading times, feature usage analytics
- **Location Data**: Approximate location (country/city) from IP for content localization

### 2.3 Third-Party Data
- **Authentication Providers**: Google, Apple (name, email, profile picture)
- **Book Data Providers**: Google Books, Open Library, Project Gutenberg, LibriVox (public book metadata)
- **Analytics**: Amplitude (anonymized usage events)
- **Crash Reporting**: Sentry (error reports with device context)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 To Provide and Improve the App
- Authenticate your account and synchronize data across devices
- Provide personalized book recommendations
- Track reading progress and synchronize across devices
- Enable offline reading and sync when online
- Generate reading statistics and achievements

### 3.2 For Communications
- Send reading reminders (with your consent)
- Notify about new releases in your favorite genres
- Send subscription-related notifications
- Respond to support requests

### 3.3 For Analytics and Improvement
- Understand how users interact with the App
- Identify and fix bugs and performance issues
- Improve features and user experience
- A/B test new features

### 3.4 For Legal and Security
- Comply with legal obligations
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Protect the rights and safety of users

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

### 4.1 Service Providers
We use trusted third-party services that process data on our behalf:
- **Supabase**: Database, authentication, storage, real-time sync
- **RevenueCat**: Subscription management
- **Amplitude**: Analytics (anonymized)
- **Sentry**: Error tracking
- **Expo**: Push notifications, build services

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

### 4.2 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental request.

### 4.3 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred.

### 4.4 With Your Consent
We may share information for social features (e.g., sharing highlights) with your explicit consent.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion
- **Reading Progress**: Retained while account is active
- **Highlights/Notes**: Retained while account is active
- **Analytics Data**: Aggregated and anonymized after 13 months
- **Crash Reports**: Retained for 90 days
- **Subscription Records**: Retained for 7 years for tax/legal compliance

You can request deletion of your data at any time through the App settings.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction**: Limit how we process your data
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
- **Objection**: Object to certain processing activities
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent for non-essential processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@bookwise.app or use the in-app data export/deletion tools.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us immediately.

## 8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Secure authentication with token-based sessions
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Principle of least privilege for data access
- Secure key management for encryption keys

## 7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU data transfers
- Adequacy decisions where applicable
- Binding Corporate Rules for intra-group transfers

## 8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale (we don't sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

## 8. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

EU residents have additional rights under GDPR:
- Lawful basis for processing (consent, contract, legitimate interest)
- Data Protection Officer: dpo@bookwise.app
- Right to lodge complaint with supervisory authority

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
